# What is this

This is an unofficial, unasked *compendium* to David Tong's [book series](https://www.davidtong.org/textbooks/) on theoretical physics. Here, I gathered reflections and notes on topics I needed to clarify or that helped enhancing my understanding. Some notes contain “exercises” on the topic. These are exercises I made up and aren't in any way provided, suggested, or reviewed by the book's author. 

I'm not a physics professore or practitioner, nor an expert in any of the topics and the content of this compendium may thus contain errors or incorrect information. I'm grateful to anybody who will help me improve the content together with my understanding of the topics.

I tend to use AI sparingly, but I find it an useful tool to interact with when I'm not sure about something or as a last resort when I'm struggling with some concept. Therefore, some of the content presented here may be a re-elaboration of AI output. What I can assure is that none of the pages here are blindly copy-and-paste from some LLM, I don't see the point with that. This is a project form me and not for others, I see no point in producing tons of content without me truly understanding it.
